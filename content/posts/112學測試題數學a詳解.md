$$(s_{1},logt_{1}),(s_{2},logt_{2})\cdots (s_{15},logt_{15})$$

![](https://mathmanstudio.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Inked擷取.jpg)

$$\frac{\bigtriangleup y}{\bigtriangleup x}=\frac{1}{2}$$

$$\frac{1}{2}=\frac{logt}{s}\to logt=\frac{s}{2}$$

$$10^{\frac{s}{2}}=t\to10^{s}=t^{2}$$
